# Solvestay Logo

**What it is:** A small house icon inside a rounded gold square.

**Colors:**
- Background: gold `#C79A4A` (primary)
- House: deep green `#083B3A` (accent)

**Meaning:** Home, trust, and finding the right place to stay.

**In the app:** Gold rounded box with a home icon, next to the word **Solvestay** in bold gold text.

**Files:** `src/app/icon.svg` and `src/app/apple-icon.svg`

**Keep it simple:** No gradients. Use the two brand colors only.
